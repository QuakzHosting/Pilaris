Terms of Service Privacy Policy Acceptable Use

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") describes rules for using Pilaris websites, control panel, and game server hosting services (the "Services"). It is part of our Terms of Service. Violations may result in suspension or termination of accounts, servers, or credits.

1. Eligibility and age

You must be at least 16 years of age to use the Services. Accounts used by individuals under 16 may be suspended or terminated.

2. Prohibited activities

You may not use the Services to engage in, facilitate, or promote any of the following:

  • Illegal activity: Any activity that violates applicable law or regulation, including distribution of illegal content, infringement of intellectual property rights, or operation of unauthorized services.
  • Malware and abuse: Hosting, distributing, or operating malware, botnets, command-and-control servers, phishing, credential theft, exploit kits, or other harmful code.
  • Network attacks and interference: Launching, participating in, or facilitating DDoS attacks, port scanning, intrusion attempts, traffic flooding, amplification, spoofing, or any activity intended to disrupt or gain unauthorized access to systems or networks.
  • Unauthorized access: Attempting to access accounts, servers, panels, data, or systems without permission; bypassing authentication, rate limits, or security controls; or probing for vulnerabilities except as explicitly authorized.
  • Resource abuse: Cryptocurrency mining, blockchain validation, CPU/GPU-intensive workloads unrelated to permitted game hosting, proxy/VPN resale, file hosting/dumping, or any use that unfairly consumes shared resources or degrades service for others.
  • Harmful or abusive content: Content that promotes violence, terrorism, child sexual abuse or exploitation, harassment, hate, doxxing, or non-consensual intimate imagery. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and will report it to appropriate authorities where required.
  • Spam and fraud: Sending spam, operating scams, abusing offerwalls or credit systems, creating multiple accounts to evade limits or bans, or using stolen/fraudulent identities or payment/offer methods.
  • Circumvention: Evading suspensions, bans, resource limits, or geographic/technical restrictions.

3. Game servers and user communities

You are responsible for the operation of your servers, installed software/mods/plugins, and the behavior of players and sub-users you invite. You must comply with the terms and licenses of the games and software you run. You must take reasonable steps to moderate your communities and prevent prohibited activities on servers you control.

4. Fair use and service limits

Because Pilaris is a free, shared hosting platform, we may apply fair-use limits on CPU, memory, disk, network, backup frequency, number of servers, or credit usage to maintain stability. We may throttle, suspend, or remove servers that negatively impact platform performance or other users, even if not explicitly listed above.

5. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and take action at our discretion, including warnings, removal of content, revocation of credits, temporary suspension, or permanent termination of accounts and servers. In urgent cases (such as active attacks, malware, or legal risk), we may act without prior notice. We may preserve and disclose information to comply with legal obligations or protect safety.

6. Reporting abuse

To report abuse, security issues, or violations of this AUP, please contact us through the official Pilaris support or community channels listed on our website. Include relevant details (such as server ID, timestamps, IPs, or evidence) to help us investigate.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, provide notice through the Services. Continued use of the Services after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
